Design uplift

Same five screens, same seed data, same engine strings — rebuilt around hierarchy and a one-owner colour contract. Tap an exercise to open it, pick an RIR and watch the chip change from the engine's assumption to your observation, cycle a pain flag, log a set. Every number is example data.

What changed, and why

Brass now means one thing

The engine's voice was #C8963E. "Under target", "warmup set" and "niggle pain" were #D9A441 — ΔE00 4.5 apart, which is close enough that most people read them as one colour. The product's central claim is that a brass number is an instruction rather than a record; that claim was being carried by a hue with three other jobs.

Now brass is the engine and nothing else. Warm is reserved for pain and nothing else. Volume reads from geometry, so it needs no hue at all.

Also fixed: the constraint colour sat ΔE00 10.3 from ordinary secondary text, so "a physio limit applies" looked like a caption. Warming the grey — one token — moved it to 30.6.

The engine's guess no longer impersonates your answer

RIR was pre-selected at target and styled exactly like a value you had chosen. So the default path — tap Log — recorded an assumption as an observation, and mean RIR is the one input the whole progression engine runs on. It is the entire difference between consolidate and progress_load.

Still one tap. But the assumed value is a brass dashed outline and a chosen one is a solid chalk fill, and the label above says which you are looking at. The card tells the truth about where the number came from — and so can the coach.

One exercise open at a time

Seven expanded cards made about 4,000px of scroll, and the Log button landed wherever the scroll left it. Collapsed rows put the session on one screen, keep the button in roughly the same place every set, and let the open card be more generous rather than less.

The volume range is visible now

The target boundaries were 1.5px hairlines measuring 1.20:1 and 2.48:1 against their track. The chart's only job is "am I inside the range" and the range could not be seen. It is a band you can see, so under/in/over reads from geometry — colour reinforces it instead of carrying it.

And a framing change: "under" was amber, a warning. Mid-week you are under target because it is Wednesday. Encoding "not finished yet" as "wrong" makes the screen scold you five days out of seven.

Rest names what's next

The timer said which set you had just finished. It now says which one is coming, so the ninety seconds are preparation rather than a gap — and it is large enough to read from arm's length with a bar to gauge without counting.

Type does less shouting

Three families became two, and eighteen sizes became eight. Mono is now reserved for measured quantities, so a number always looks like a number and a label never does. That one rule is most of what stopped the card reading as a wall of data.

Contrast: the "kg" unit beside each prescription measured 2.25:1. Everything here clears AA, and controls clear the 3:1 non-text floor.

Left alone on purpose

Dark-only, the engine's rationale strings, the information architecture, the five-tab nav and the seed data are all unchanged. They were the strongest parts of the original and none of the problems above came from them.
