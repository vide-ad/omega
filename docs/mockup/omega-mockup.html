Omega

Interactive mockup

The training log's five screens, running on the real seed data and the real engine output. Tap the RIR chips, cycle a pain flag, log a set and watch the rest timer start. Every number below is example data — a second week of the seed block, not a session you've logged.

What to look at

The brass numbers are the engine talking

Anything in brass is a prescription, not a record. Tap WHY on any exercise to see the reason and the rationale — the same strings the coaching API returns. Nothing is hidden from you or from the coach.

Bench says hold, not add

You hit 10, 10, 10 last week — Strong would add weight. The engine holds at 42.5 kg because your mean RIR was 2 against a target of 3: the reps are there but the effort cost is still too high. Reason consolidate.

The injury is enforced, not remembered

The curl is capped at 5 kg with a 15-rep floor and a 3-0-3-0 tempo, and the chin-up won't take a load at all until physio clears it. Both carry the physio's note. The engine cannot prescribe past either.

One tap per set

RIR is a chip row with your target pre-selected, never a modal. Pain is one button cycling four states. Both sit on the row you're already touching.

Squat is unstarted on purpose

No qualifying history and no stored starting load, so it asks rather than guessing. That's first_time — the one case where the engine declines to prescribe.
